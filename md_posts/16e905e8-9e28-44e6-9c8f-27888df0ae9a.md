## 1.3 Prosody & Sandhi

### Stress

- Typically falls on the first syllable.
- Some noun prefixes are unstressed.
- In verbs, prefixes are unstressed unless adverbial.

### Syllable Weight

A syllable ending in a short vowel (or a short-nucleotic, codaless syllable) is **light**.

A sequence of a light stressed syllable followed by another syllable is equivalent to a heavy syllable in poetry.

### Inner Sandhi

- Palatal and velar sounds revert to velars before obstruents.
- Nasals assimilate to the place of articulation of the following sound.
- Two identical vowels combine into a long vowel.

Example:

```
mani + itam → maniitam
```

The language is highly inflected. Nouns decline for:

# 3. Verb Morphology

Verbs are agglutinative.

Order of suffixes:

```
[Mood/Tense] + [Aspect] + [Person/Number] + [Negation]
```

---

## 3.1 Stem Formation

There are four primary ways to form a verb stem.

### Type 1

Root = Stem (base).

### Type 2

Root + reduplication.

### Type 3

Nasal infix before the final consonant.

### Type 4

`nu-` added to the root.

# 7. Syntax

## 7.1 Relative Clauses (Compounded)

Relative clauses are formed by transforming the verb into a **gendered participle** using the nominalization suffixes and compounding it directly with the head noun.

The participle functions as the first element of an **endocentric compound**, qualifying the noun that follows.

### Structure

```text
[Clause Content] + [Verb-Suffix] + [Head Noun]
```

### Rules

- The verb takes the gender/number suffix matching the head noun.
- The participle and the noun are compounded (or hyphenated) into a single semantic unit.
- Relative clauses precede the noun they modify.

### Participle Agreement

| Head Noun Gender | Participle Suffix | Example |
|------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Masculine | `-yaar` | `vun-ām ɲa-yaar kʲer` ("the bird-seeing man") |
| Feminine | `-i` | `vun-ām ɲa-i vun` ("the bird-seeing bird") |
| Neuter | `-aan` | `vun-ām ɲa-aan ɸir` ("the bird-seeing eye") |

### Example

```text
vun-ām ɲa-yaar kʲer t͜ɕa-au

bird-ACC see-MASC.PART-man stand-PRES

"The bird-seeing man stands."
```

---

## 7.2 Complement Clauses

Complement clauses (clauses functioning as the object of a verb, such as **"I know that..."**) are formed using the subordinating particle **tɬom**.

### Structure

```text
[Subordinate Subject] + [Subordinate Verb-tɬom] + [Main Verb]
```

### Rules

- The subordinate clause retains normal finite verbal morphology.
- The particle **-tɬom** is appended to the subordinate verb.
- The entire subordinate clause functions as the object of the main verb.

### Example

```text
Sa tɬo-au-tɬom t͜ɕeθ-mi

sa      tɬo-au-tɬom      t͜ɕeθ-mi
3SG     sleep-PRES-THAT  know-1SG

"I know that she is sleeping."
```

---

## 7.3 Constituent Order (SOV)

The language follows a canonical **Subject–Object–Verb (SOV)** word order.

### Relative Clauses

Because relative clauses are compounded with the noun, they occupy the same syntactic position as the noun phrase.

```text
[Subject] + [Object (Relative Compound)] + [Verb]
```

### Complement Clauses

Complement clauses function as the object of the main verb and therefore precede the main verb.

```text
[Subject] + [Complement Clause] + [Main Verb]
```

### Summary

| Construction | Word Order |
|--------------|------------|
| Simple Clause | Subject → Object → Verb |
| Relative Clause | Subject → Relative Compound → Verb |
| Complement Clause | Subject → Complement Clause → Main Verb |

# Sample Glossed Texts

The following examples use only (or almost entirely) the current lexicon and follow the language's SOV structure.

These examples use full noun case marking and finite verbal agreement according to the grammar.

---

## 1. The Great Sun Gives Water to the Earth.

### Conlang

```text
Gʲom ŋon-æθ ŋoɬ-i vif-au-ti.
```

### Gloss

```text
gʲom     ŋon-æθ      ŋoɬ-i       vif-au-ti
great    sun-NOM     earth-DAT   give-PRES-3SG
```

### Translation

> *The great Sun gives unto the Earth.*

# Hymn of the Dawn

### Conlang

```text
Gʲom ŋon-æθ
ɲet-æp vif-au-ti.

ɬaʃ-æθ tɬoŋ-æp ɬo-au-ti.

Praver kʲer-æθ
θæɲ-æp ɲa-au-ti,
ŋon-erθ breyn-æp
t͜ɕeθ-au-ti.
```

### Gloss

```text
gʲom     ŋon-æθ
great    sun-NOM

ɲet-æp      vif-au-ti
water-ACC   give-PRES-3SG

ɬaʃ-æθ      tɬoŋ-æp      ɬo-au-ti
fire-NOM    night-ACC    consume-PRES-3SG

praver      kʲer-æθ
wise         man-NOM

θæɲ-æp      ɲa-au-ti
sky-ACC     see-PRES-3SG

ŋon-erθ      breyn-æp
sun-GEN      strength-ACC

t͜ɕeθ-au-ti
know-PRES-3SG
```

### Translation

> *Great is the Sun,
> who gives the waters.
>
> Fire consumes the night.
>
> The wise man beholds the heavens
> and knows the strength of the Sun.*
[^1]: Free Word Order with a tendency for SOV
